University Overview:
Name: Ken42
Tagline: University of Tomorrow
Description: Established through the Ken42 Act, two thousand three, of the State Legislature of Karnataka, Ken42 is a top-ranked, UGC-recognised, private university. Ken42 envisions, designs, and delivers education that is not just relevant for today, but also anticipates the challenges and opportunities of the future, moulding students into leaders who will make a positive impact on the world.
Core Philosophy: Transformative education, cultivating a dynamic learning environment that transcends conventional boundaries, with a robust emphasis on digital preparedness and cutting-edge skills.
Global Exposure Initiatives: Partnerships with prestigious institutions worldwide including University of California in Berkeley, The University of Edinburgh, UNSW Sydney, The University of Queensland, King’s College London, University of Gothenburg, University of Bologna, University of Aberdeen. Global Immersion Program for experiential learning.
Schools Offering B.Tech: School of Advanced Engineering (SOAE), School of Computer Science (SOCS)

Academic Environment:
  Design Your Own Degree:
  Concept: A holistic curriculum framework focusing on individual student needs to discover, experience, explore, and challenge.
  Components:
    - Core Subject Studies
    - Core Specialism Studies (one hundred fifty plus specializations across seven schools)
    - Minors/Exploratory Subjects
    - Signature and Life Skills Learning (offered by School for Life)
  Internships: Mandatory internships in social sector, government/public sector, and industry.

School for Life (SFL):
  Description: An intrinsic part of the Ken42 student experience, facilitating a balance between what students want and what is needed for them as future global citizens and leaders. Focuses on lifelong learning skills, contemporary issues, and a mandatory social/professional internship.
  Offerings:
    - Signature Courses: Focus on twenty-first-century learner skills and attributes, empowering students as global citizens. Undertaken by all UG and select graduate students.
    - Exploratory Courses (Interdisciplinary Electives): Encourage a broad-based approach by selecting subjects from other Ken42 schools, growing breadth and depth of knowledge beyond chosen specialization.
    - Life Skill Courses: Upskill in university learning styles, enhance employability, communication, management, professional skills, critical thinking, problem-solving, and creativity.
    - 'Srijan' Social Internships: Full-time, eight-week-long internships with social sector organisations for all first-year students. Builds empathy and leadership skills.

      - Passed without any supplementary exam. If student fails to obtain minimum C G P A of eight point five or has an active backlog, scholarship discontinued and can only be renewed in next academic year subject to meeting eligibility criteria.
      - Awarded on the basis of academic grades and attendance, post declaration of results on a yearly basis. E.g., scholarship based on first year result reflects in second year fee invoice (third semester), benefit reflects in fourth semester.

      - Awarded as Fee Concession; Fee Discount; Fee Waiver on tuition fees only. Cannot be exchanged for cash/cheque or transferred.
